Simplified confocal scan head

From excitation to detected signal

The dichroic reflects the scanned excitation beam into the objective, which focuses it to one spot. Fluorescence leaves that spot in all directions; the objective accepts only its numerical-aperture cone, and the dichroic transmits that return signal to the detector.
